About

Melodic house music
straight from Austria.

Skili, originally from Tyrol, is a Vienna-based DJ and music producer with a background in piano, jazz, pop, rock and electronic music. After completing his studies in music production, he began focusing on developing his own sound and performing as a DJ. His music ranges from various styles of House to melodic electronic music, combining his production knowledge with innovative live sets. Skili has held residencies at renowned Vienna venues such as Babenberger Passage, Hannelore and Kleinod am Ring, and has performed at clubs including U4 and O-Klub. He has established himself within the Austrian DJ scene, sharing lineups with internationally acclaimed artists such as Sam Feldt, Alle Farben, Faul & Wad, Eran Hersh, Vanco, Kurd Maverick and Divolly & Markward.
